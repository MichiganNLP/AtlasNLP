Key findings

What AtlasNLP reveals

59.7%
of country-task pairs are empty

Even countries with datasets tend to be concentrated in a handful of tasks like machine translation and sentiment analysis — leaving most of the NLP task space uncovered.

39%
of datasets come from just 5 countries

The top five countries dominate NLP dataset production. Meanwhile, 101 countries have 25 or fewer datasets each — and 64 countries have 10 or fewer.

33.8%
of datasets lack country attribution

Language metadata is an incomplete proxy for geography. A language appearing across many countries doesn't mean balanced coverage — French datasets are dominated by France; Spanish by Spain.
